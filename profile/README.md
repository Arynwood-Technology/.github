## What we build

**[Arynwood MCP](https://github.com/Arynwood-Technology/ArynwoodMCP)** a local-first
AI creative studio for Linux. Multi-persona AI chat, GPU-powered generation (image,
voice, video, music), a knowledge base, and publishing tools, all running on your own
hardware. Currently in **Linux alpha**.

## Why local-first

Your models, your projects, and your data on hardware you control. Optional features
that reach outside your machine (web search, publishing integrations) are exactly
that: optional, and disclosed when they're used.
